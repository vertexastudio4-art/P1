Never lose a patient to a missed call again

A complete AI system for clinics: WhatsApp booking, confirmation calls, reminders, review requests and a CRM, all in one place.

See how it worksThe problem

The real-world problem

Clinics get patients through calls and WhatsApp, but nobody has time to follow up on every single one.

Missed calls

Calls go unanswered when the receptionist is busy or the clinic is closed.

Forgotten appointments

Patients forget their slot, so the doctor’s time is wasted.

Few reviews

Happy patients rarely leave Google reviews, so new patients never find the clinic.

Manual tracking

Bookings sit in registers and chats, and nothing is tracked in one place.

How the system works

One simple flow, fully automated from the first message to the final follow-up.

1

Patient reaches out

By call, WhatsApp or the website.

2

WhatsApp bot books the slot

Instant reply, 24/7, in Hindi or English.

3

AI voice agent confirms

A short call confirms the appointment.

4

Reminder before the visit

Automatic WhatsApp reminder reduces no-shows.

5

Review request after the visit

A friendly message asks for a Google review.

6

CRM updates itself

Every step is logged for the clinic owner.
